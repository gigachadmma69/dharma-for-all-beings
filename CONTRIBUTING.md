# Care for the teachings and the readers

The aim is reliable access to teachings that invite understanding and compassion. No excerpt, ordering, posting rate or algorithm can certify liberation or objectively rank benefit to all beings. Concision must not strip away meaning.

## Propose a passage

Supply the actual speaker, exact wording, work, translator or interpreter, edition, precise locator, authoritative source link, surrounding context and reuse basis. Distinguish a historical attribution, treasure revelation, visionary dialogue, modern recollection and actual recording. A teacher named in a title is not necessarily speaking. Auto-captions and search snippets are discovery aids only.

Before approval, check both exact and near-duplicate passages across archive.json, media.json and reserve.json. Count use cumulatively by underlying work even when reproduced at several URLs. Preserve translator wording; do not stitch, modernize or paraphrase inside a quotation. Link complete teachings rather than copying them serially. A second reader should review a difficult attribution or contextual ambiguity before release.

Favor self-contained teachings that connect insight with kindness and responsible conduct. Vary traditions, women and men, forms of practice and levels of complexity. Prioritizing Padmasambhava, Yeshe Tsogyal and Erik Pema Kunsang must not override provenance or turn a plural library into a claim that one formulation serves every reader.

## Correct or withdraw

A correction report should identify the entry ID, its source and the evidence of the problem. Do not include private information in public reports. Caretakers must stop selecting a disputed passage, inspect the original context, and record a dated decision with supporting evidence. Do not quietly rewrite a quotation to make it sound better.

For an established error, correct or remove the entry from the reading sequence, reserve and feed, rebuild and publish, and inspect pending social posts for the same error. Correct published posts where supported and appropriate. Record old and new wording, affected entry IDs and the reason in a public correction note without republishing excessive copyrighted text. Superseded releases should receive a notice pointing to the corrected edition; an immutable old copy may persist elsewhere.

For a reuse complaint, pause further use while reviewing the claim and source rights. The MIT software license does not cover translations. Do not treat a public webpage as permission for unlimited extraction.

## Keep the system honest

Collected candidates, reviewed excerpts, eligible reserve, scheduled posts and confirmed publications are different counts. A successful build does not verify truth or establish a live publishing service. Never claim a backup, successor, permission or independent host exists until it has been confirmed.

Run the existing preservation check before publishing and the restore test before issuing a recovery release. Keep accounts, credentials, private logs and contact details out of the repository. See CONTINUITY.md for monthly recovery and channel-specific failure handling.
